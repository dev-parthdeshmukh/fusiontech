# MNI ICBM152 non-linear symmetric 2009a template

`mni152_t1.nii.gz`, `mni152_gm.nii.gz` and `mni152_wm.nii.gz` are the 1 mm T1-weighted
template and grey/white-matter probability maps of the ICBM 2009a nonlinear symmetric
atlas, in the rescaled form distributed with [nilearn](https://nilearn.github.io).
FusionMap uses them only as the anatomical basis of its *digital phantoms*.
